

## 📊 Data Jobs Dashboard 2.0 — Power BI
*Exploring the 2024 data job market with clarity and depth. 🚀*

![Data Jobs Dashboard Preview](/images/Data_jobs_dashboard_2.jpg)




This dashboard is a focused **2024 snapshot of data-related job postings**, designed to help users quickly understand  
**job demand, skill popularity, and salary benchmarks** across roles and locations.

Built with a job-seeker mindset, it turns complex market data into **clear, actionable insights** through interactive visuals and smart filtering.

- 💼 **Most in-demand data roles**

## 🛠️ Power BI Features & Tech Used

- 📐 **Explicit DAX Measures**  
  Custom measures for job count, percentages, salary metrics, and skill aggregation.

- 🧮 **Parameters & Data Modeling**  
  Parameters used to drive dynamic views and comparisons across roles and metrics.

- 🔄 **Power Query (ETL & Data Shaping)**  
  Cleaning, transforming, and optimizing raw job-posting data for analysis.

- 🔗 **Data Modeling & Relationships**  
  Creation of new tables and **custom relationships** to enable accurate aggregations and slicing.

- 🎨 **Custom Theme & Background Design**  
  Dashboard background and visual theme designed using **PowerPoint** for a clean, consistent UI.

- 📊 **Visuals Used**
  - Bar Charts
  - Multi Card layout
  - Slicers for detailed breakdowns

> 💡 *A balance of strong modeling, smart measures, and thoughtful design.*

## 🎛️ How to Use the Dashboard

- 🎯 Use **Job Title** and **Country slicers** to focus on specific roles or regions
- 💰 Toggle between **Median Hourly** and **Median Yearly Salary**
- 📊 Switch views between **Job Count** and **Job Percentage**
- 🧠 Explore skill demand and salary insights dynamically through visual interactions
- 🧹 Click the **Clear Slicers** button *(Ctrl + Click)* to reset all filters instantly

> 👉 *Designed for fast exploration — filter, compare, reset, repeat.*

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- 💼 **Senior Data Scientist** roles command the **highest median yearly salary**, reflecting strong demand for advanced experience.
- 📉 **Data Analyst** positions show the **lowest median yearly salary**, making them a common entry point into data careers.
- ⏱️ **Machine Learning Engineers** earn the **highest median hourly salary**, highlighting the premium on specialized ML skills.
- 🧠 **Python and SQL** dominate as the most in-demand skills, reinforcing their importance across all data roles.
- 🇮🇳 **India-specific insights** show:
  - Higher **skills-per-job requirement**, indicating competitive role expectations  
  - Strong demand for engineering-heavy roles over purely analytical ones
- 🎯 Salary and skill demand vary significantly by role, making **targeted upskilling** essential for career growth.

> 🚀 *The data clearly shows: specialization pays — and the right skills unlock better opportunities.*
